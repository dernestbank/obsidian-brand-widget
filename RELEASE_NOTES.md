# Brand-widget 1.1.2

Create a recognizable workspace with your logo, motto and useful links.

- Configurable sidebar widget and status-bar logo.
- Local image picker for logos and backgrounds, with adjustable readability overlay.
- Quick links to vault notes, headings or websites.
- Optional slow spin, gentle bounce, soft dissolve and subtle float, with reduced-motion support.
- Custom plugin icon and theme-aware presentation.

Desktop release for Obsidian 1.14.5 or newer. No telemetry or background network access. Select images are copied into the vault without changing their originals.

For manual installation, extract the ZIP's brand-widget folder into your vault's .obsidian/plugins directory, restart Obsidian and enable Brand-widget.
